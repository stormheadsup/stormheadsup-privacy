StormHeadsUp — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

StormHeadsUp ("the app", "we", "us") is a weather heads-up app that shows National Weather Service (NWS/NOAA) forecasts, storm alerts, radar, and nearby FAA weather cameras. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and who it is shared with. We do not sell your personal information.

Not an official warning system. StormHeadsUp is a convenience tool. Its data and notifications can be delayed, interrupted, or wrong — never rely on it for life-safety decisions. Always follow official warnings from the National Weather Service, NOAA Weather Radio, Wireless Emergency Alerts, and your local emergency management.

Data we collect and use

  • Location. With your permission, the app uses your device's approximate and precise location to show weather, alerts, radar, and cameras for where you are. Location is accessed only while you are using the app. You can instead type a city or ZIP code and skip location entirely; the app still works. City and ZIP searches are matched against a location list bundled inside the app, so what you type never leaves your device.
  • Advertising identifier & ad data. The app displays a banner ad through Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising ID and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Technical data. When the app requests weather, map, or camera data, standard network information (such as your IP address) is visible to the services it contacts, as with any internet request.
  • Crash and diagnostic data. If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a crash report (device model, operating system version, app version, and the technical error details) is sent to Sentry, our crash-reporting provider, so we can find and fix bugs. Crash reports are not tied to any account or used for advertising.
  • On-device preferences. Your saved locations and notification settings are stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted to us or stored on any server we operate.

The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or health data. We do not operate our own servers that store your personal data.

How your location is shared to provide the service

To return weather and related data for your area, the app sends your location (as coordinates, a ZIP code, or a state) to the public services it relies on, including:

  • National Weather Service / NOAA (weather.gov, NOAA radar) — forecasts, alerts, radar
  • U.S. EPA — UV index
  • FAA Weather Camera Program — nearby weather cameras
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles for the radar view

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show a banner ad. Google may use the advertising ID and device data to provide and personalize ads and measure their performance. Where required by law (for example, in the EEA and UK), the app shows Google's consent prompt before serving ads, and you can revisit your choices anytime via Settings → Ad privacy options in the app. If your consent choice cannot be gathered (for example, you're offline), the app requests only non-personalized ads. You can learn about and control this through Google's advertising policies, and you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your Android settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Crash reporting

The app uses Sentry to collect crash reports and diagnostic events. These contain technical details about the failure and the device (model, OS version, app version). They do not include your name, email, saved locations, or advertising ID, and are used solely to keep the app reliable. See Sentry's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you enable them, the app sends weather heads-up notifications from your home location. These are generated for you and can be turned off anytime in the app or in your device settings.

Children

StormHeadsUp is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested at a family-friendly content rating.

Your choices

  • Grant, deny, or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings.
  • Use manual city/ZIP entry instead of location.
  • Opt out of personalized ads via the consent prompt (where shown), the app's Settings → Ad privacy options, and your device's ad settings.
  • Turn notifications on or off.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored preferences.

Data retention & security

Your preferences remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Data sent to the services above is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and retained by those services under their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
